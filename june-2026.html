🌹 Sister Spotlight
Congratulations, Jaynellen! 🥂🌹
Women in Motion Luncheon · Fri, June 26 · The Pierre, NYC
Members of Alpha Omicron Pi have long admired Jaynellen Behre Jenkins' unwavering dedication to our sisterhood. Beyond her extensive volunteer service, Jaynellen served three terms as an International Vice President, held a distinguished tenure on the Executive Board, and currently serves as the Chapter Advisor for the Theta Pi chapter at Wagner College.

This June, the Arthritis Foundation will honor Jaynellen at the Women in Motion Luncheon on Friday, June 26th from 12:30–2:30 PM at The Pierre in New York City. For those unable to attend the luncheon, we are planning a celebratory gathering nearby, with additional details to follow. We hope you will join us in recognizing Jaynellen for this well-deserved honor.
  📅 When: Friday, June 26 · 12:30–2:30 PM
📍 Where: The Pierre, New York City
🌐 More info: arthritis.org/womeninmotionnyc

Team NYC AOII · Foley Square
