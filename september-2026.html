🌟 New! Collegiate Mentor Roster
Support Our Collegiate Sisters
In an effort to better support our local collegiate chapters, we're starting a mentor roster so collegiate women can get in contact with alumnae. When you sign up, you can indicate:
  Your preferred method of contact
  Whether you're open to in-person or virtual connection
  What you're willing to offer — mentorship, career advice, or special skills (crafting, fitness instruction, etc.)
Any level of support is appreciated — no time commitment is too small!
If you'd like to be added to the roster, email nycaoiialum@gmail.com.
