Theta Pi's 75th Anniversary 🌹
Wagner College
Barbara Woods and Lizzy Klein made the trip to Theta Pi chapter's 75th anniversary celebration at Wagner College. Their chapter advisor Jaynellen and the actives pulled off a beautiful ceremony and a seriously good luncheon — with AOII's international president Susan Bonifield in attendance and alumnae going back 67 years in the room. A pretty incredible afternoon of sisterhood!

Letters of Recommendation ✉️
It's that season
Each year around this time, our chapter starts getting requests from potential new members going through Fall recruitment who don't know any AOII alumnae personally and are taking a shot in the dark. They'll typically offer to send a resume or meet online. Heather fields these through social media; I get some on the chapter email. If you're open to participating, sign up here — we'll always ask before connecting you with anyone.
