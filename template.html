2026 Annual Dues 🌹
Annual dues support NYC AOII alumnae programming, sisterhood events, and communications — and help us stay connected all year.

2026 Dues Options:
  • $45 — First Year: For any member who’s never paid alumnae dues and graduated 2021 or later.
  • $49 — Life Loyal: AOII Life Loyal members pay discounted chapter dues for life.
  • $75 — Regular Member: Standard annual dues for NYC AOII Alumnae Chapter members.
  • $18.97 — Secondary Member: You must be a member of another AOII alumnae chapter to be eligible.
