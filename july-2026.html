A Note From Our President
From Lizzy 🌹
Hi from Vermont!

By now you may have heard that I've made a big move — after 35ish years in NYC, I relocated to Brattleboro, Vermont in June. It's gorgeous up here and I love having space and being near family. Fredi the Wonder Dog loves having woods right outside the front door and met her first porcupine last night (and I met the crack team at the emergency vet hospital!)

But what about NYC AOII? The exec board and I talked this through and they asked me to finish out my term as chapter president from here, which I'm glad to do. Thanks to how much of our chapter business runs digitally, this won't change too much except I'll be sad to miss seeing your faces at Toast on the 10th! But I'm in for book group, hoping to make Friendsgiving in person, and I will do my best to show up for the chapter as best I can.

The fresh air and endless green are recharging me, and being here hasn't changed how much this chapter means to me. I'm so proud of what this chapter continues to build, and I'm not going anywhere in terms of my commitment to you or AOII. Please message me with any questions.

And if you're ever up this way, please come visit Fredi and me in the blue barn!

Roses,
Lizzy

🌹 Recap
Women in Motion Luncheon 🥂🌹
Honoring Jaynellen Behre Jenkins · Fri, June 26 · The Pierre, NYC
The Arthritis Foundation New York's Women in Motion luncheon was a tremendous success — and a proud moment for AOII. Our honoree, Jaynellen Behre Jenkins, received her award from AOII International President Susan Bonifeld, and brought down the house when she revealed that AOII has made another $1,000,000 gift to the Arthritis Foundation — an audible gasp from the room said it all.

Jaynellen didn't just fundraise — she also organized an effort to bring collegians to the event, so a new audience could see the AOII–Arthritis partnership in action. Other notable attendees included actress Kathleen Turner. And at the after-luncheon celebration, we discovered that several other attendees are AOIIs who are now eager to get involved with NYC AOII!

Never underestimate a group of determined women with a shared purpose. 🌹
