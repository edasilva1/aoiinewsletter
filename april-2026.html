Alumnae Leadership Conference 🌹
Open to All AOII Alumnae
April 17–18, 2026 · Virtual
AOII's 6th annual Alumnae Leadership Conference is a virtual event designed to inspire, connect, and empower alumnae across North America. This year's keynote, "Ready Enough" by Lauren Beane, focuses on pursuing your goals without waiting for perfect readiness. The conference features curated education tracks, networking, and professional development — and it's open to all alumnae!
