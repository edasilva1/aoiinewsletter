📚 This Month's Book
Yesteryear
by Caro Claire Burke
Yesteryear by Caro Claire Burke is a 2026 debut novel about a "tradwife" social media influencer who wakes up in 1855, forced to confront the harsh reality of the 19th-century life she romanticized online, leading to a satirical and suspenseful story about fame, faith, and the performance of womanhood. The book follows her struggle to survive without modern conveniences while questioning if her situation is a hoax, a reality show, or something more sinister, exploring the gap between curated online personas and reality.

Friends and family are welcome — bring someone new!
